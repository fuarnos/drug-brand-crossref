## 英文正文

```
Subject: Request for access to the "의약품 제품 허가정보" dataset (data.go.kr #15095677) as a foreign organisation

Dear Sir or Madam,

We operate https://drugcrossref.com/ , a non-commercial reference site that helps users
identify a medicine by its brand name across countries. We only map identities: product name,
active ingredient(s) and strength, dosage form, marketing-authorisation holder and registration
number. We do not provide dosing advice and we do not recommend substitutions.

We would like to add the Republic of Korea to our cross-reference using the public dataset
"식품의약품안전처_의약품 제품 허가정보" (data.go.kr dataset #15095677), whose stated licence is
"이용허락 제한 없음" (no restriction on use).

The obstacle is account registration: a personal account requires Korean mobile-phone
real-name verification, and a corporate account requires a business registration number
registered with the National Tax Service. Our organisation is not registered in Korea, so we
cannot complete either path.

Could you advise on one of the following?
 (a) issuing a service key for the OpenAPI (or a bulk CSV/XML file) to a foreign organisation
     without Korean identity verification, or
 (b) a written permission / data-sharing arrangement under which we may use the factual fields
     listed above.

We would store only factual fields, attribute the source as 식품의약품안전처 (MFDS) with the
dataset's last-update date, and publish no dosing or substitution information.

Thank you for your time and for maintaining this public dataset.

Best regards,
<name / organisation>
<email>
```
