Warnings: same name, different drug

Published warnings about names that are easily confused, or products that are not interchangeable — across countries, salt forms or release mechanisms. This page only flags identity and interchangeability; it contains no dosing advice and no substitution recommendations.

Not medical advice. Check the actual pack and the ingredient, and ask a pharmacist before changing products.

High Aminophylline and theophylline use different strength bases

Aminophylline is a theophylline-ethylenediamine complex (about 80% theophylline by mass); label strengths may refer to either. Narrow therapeutic index — no milligram-for-milligram substitution.
