Weight-loss medicines

ATC A08 + A10BJ (GLP-1) + A10BX16 (tirzepatide) — antiobesity preparations and GLP-1 weight-loss medicines. GLP-1s also belong to the diabetes class A10; this is a browse-by-indication collection, not a claim of equivalence.
ATC A08 + A10BJ (GLP-1) + A10BX16 (tirzepatida) — preparados antiobesidad y medicamentos GLP-1 para perder peso. Los GLP-1 también pertenecen a la clase de diabetes A10; es una colección por indicación, no una afirmación de equivalencia.
ATC A08 + A10BJ（GLP-1）+ A10BX16（tirzepatide）——传统抗肥胖药与 GLP-1 减重药。GLP-1 同属降糖大类 A10；这里按减重适应症浏览，不代表同效或可互换。
ATC A08 + A10BJ（GLP-1）+ A10BX16（tirzepatide）——傳統抗肥胖藥與 GLP-1 減重藥。GLP-1 同屬降糖大類 A10；這裡按減重適應症瀏覽，不代表同效或可互換。
GLP-1 medicines such as semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide, dulaglutide and exenatide are also listed under Diabetes medicines (A10). The same ingredient appears in both categories by indication — not as an interchangeable alternative.

Notes & licences
Status badges summarise the product records held for each ingredient (active / discontinued / withdrawn / unknown), and the market codes after each badge show where that status is recorded. Only some registers publish a status column: CA, ES and MX do; AU and HK have no status column and are recorded as unknown; CN, US, GB, TW and SG records are on the register and marked active.
Class and category names are editorial or AI-assisted translations; only the ATC code and the WHO hierarchy are authoritative.
ATC codes and hierarchy reference the WHO Collaborating Centre for Drug Statistics Methodology ATC/DDD Index (copyright WHOCC; the index itself is not reproduced). Ingredient/strength facts come from the regulator records listed on About.
