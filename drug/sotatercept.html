“not disclosed” means the source does not publish the strength — it does not mean the product has none.

Hong Kong records are name/ingredient-level only: no strength, dosage form or registration status; on the register does not prove current supply.

Australia ARTG data has no registration-status column, so status is shown as unknown; being on the register does not mean currently marketed.

The same ingredient can carry completely different brand names in different countries, and the same name can point to a different ingredient. Listed by country — not interchangeable.

Discontinued, withdrawn or unverified-status products are hidden by default; Hong Kong and Australia records are shown as exceptions.
